## 🚀 About the Project

**NAAM & Health Care** is a healthcare-oriented project developed for **SGDC HACKATHON 2K26**. It explores how digital technology can help make healthcare information more accessible, improve health awareness, and support people in navigating healthcare resources.

Our goal is to explore practical, inclusive technology that puts people and their well-being first.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Access to understandable health information and appropriate healthcare resources can be challenging for individuals and communities. Digital tools may help reduce information barriers and make health-related resources easier to discover and use.

## 💡 Our Proposed Approach

- 🩺 **Health Awareness:** Make general health information easier to understand.
- 📱 **Digital Accessibility:** Explore a simple, user-friendly healthcare interface.
- 👥 **Patient Support:** Consider tools that help users navigate available healthcare resources.
- 📋 **Information Organization:** Present relevant healthcare information clearly.
- 🌍 **Community Impact:** Explore ways technology can support healthier communities.

*These are proposed objectives, not claims that these features are already implemented.*

## ✨ Project Objectives

1. Explore practical technology solutions for healthcare challenges.
2. Improve the accessibility and clarity of health-related information.
3. Design an intuitive experience for users with different levels of digital literacy.
4. Consider privacy and responsible handling of personal information.
5. Develop and demonstrate a prototype during SGDC HACKATHON 2K26.

## 🔒 Privacy, Safety & Responsibility

- Protect personal and medical information.
- Collect only the data necessary for the intended purpose.
- Do not present unverified information as medical advice.
- Encourage users to consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical concerns.
- Design with accessibility, usability, and responsible data handling in mind.
